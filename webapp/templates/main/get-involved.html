How to get involved

There are so many ways to bring Christmas to a child in foster care.

  • Host a Holiday Hope Tree by emailing {{ config['CONTACT_EMAIL'] }}.
  • Click here to sponsor a foster child’s wishlist.
  • Gift cards! This helps purchase gifts for kids that come into care right before Christmas. Walmart, Amazon and Target are great examples. Please email e-gift cards to {{ config['CONTACT_EMAIL'] }}.
  • We have created an Amazon wish list of popular items! These will ship directly to the church where foster families will be receiving their Christmas gifts. Amazon Wishlist.
  • Your church or business could host a toy drive!
  • Your church or business could hold a gift card drive for teens that have aged out of foster care (aged 18-21).
  • Volunteer! We will need so much help the week of this event to receive and sort all gifts. Please register to volunteer here.
  • Sponsorships! Email us for more info on sponsorships through our 501(c)(3).
