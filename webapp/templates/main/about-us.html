Our Story

About Holiday Hope Tree

Who we are

Holiday Hope Tree was born from a simple, heartfelt realization: every child deserves to feel the magic of Christmas morning. Our journey began in 2021 when two of our founding members, both foster parents, saw firsthand how deeply the Department of Human Services (DHS) cared for children in the system—but also how desperately they lacked the time and resources to provide holiday gifts. Determined to bridge that gap, a small group of family, friends, and local churches came together to help. What started as a modest effort to support a few hundred local foster youth quickly blossomed. To meet an ever-growing need, we officially established Holiday Hope Tree as a non-profit organization dedicated to bringing help, hope, and joy to children, teens, and young adults in foster care across Pulaski County.

What we do

Every holiday season, “That Church” in Sherwood, Arkansas, generously opens its doors, donating their entire facility to serve as our festive headquarters. Volunteers transform the space into a bustling workshop filled with dedicated "elves" and piles of gifts. We believe that every child is unique, so their gifts should be too. Through our online system, we work closely with DHS caseworkers and foster parents to gather specific, personalized wish lists from each child. Whether it’s a specific doll with a certain hair color, correct clothing and shoe sizes, or a long-awaited toy, our sponsors strive to fulfill these precise wishes. We ensure that every gift bundle is deeply personal, sending a message to these children that they are individually known and loved.

It truly takes a village

The holidays can be an incredibly difficult time for a child separated from their biological family. Days that should be filled with pure excitement are often veiled with loss and uncertainty. Because state funding does not cover holiday gifts, and monthly foster stipends rarely stretch to cover extras, Holiday Hope Tree steps in to support both the children and the generous foster families who open their hearts and homes. We cannot do this work alone. We are a community-wide organization that proudly welcomes partners from all backgrounds and beliefs. Our mission is sustained by a beautiful village: local churches of all denominations, small businesses, and community organizations. We also partner with The Call to provide convenient gift drop-off locations for our supporters across the region. We are united by a single, unwavering goal: to ensure that children in foster care feel seen, special, and deeply valued.

How you can help

Our community consistently astounds us with its generosity, wrapping our local foster youth in love year after year. There are many ways you can be a part of this mission:

  • Host a Tree: Churches, businesses, and local events can host a Holiday Hope Tree filled with custom wish lists, allowing individuals to shop directly for a specific child.
  • Toy & Gift Card Drives: Donations of new toys and gift cards are crucial to helping us supplement lists and fulfill last-minute requests.
  • Financial Contributions & Corporate Sponsorships: Financial gifts allow us to purchase larger, pricier items like bicycles and tablets, and provide essential support for young adults (ages 18-21) who are aging out of care. Funds also allow us to quickly shop for children who enter the foster system unexpectedly between our main pickup deadline and Christmas Day.

Every donation, large or small, ensures that a child in our community wakes up to a beautiful Christmas morning. Thank you for opening your heart to give the gift of hope.
