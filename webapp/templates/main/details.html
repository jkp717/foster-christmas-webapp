For foster parents

  • This website is for children based out of Pulaski or Lonoke County DHS only. If you live outside of those counties but your child is from a Pulaski or Lonoke County office you will still register here.
  • If you live in Pulaski or Lonoke county and have a child from another county please contact your case-worker from that county about how they are handling Christmas, every county is a little different.
  • Gift pick up will be for {{ lib.render_pickup_locations(config) }}. We would love to meet you and thank you in person but if your time absolutely does not work, please make sure to click the box “Will need DHS to deliver” when registering your child so that we can get that arranged for you.
  • Deadline for the child’s wish lists is {{ child_signup_deadline or 'TBD' }}.
